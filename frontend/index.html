Why Earthquake Prediction Matters

Earthquakes cannot be fully prevented, but better risk estimation can reduce damage by improving early warning, evacuation planning, emergency deployment, and infrastructure response.

2015 Nepal Earthquake

Major infrastructure collapse and large human loss highlighted gaps in readiness and hazard communication.

2023 Turkey-Syria Earthquakes

Severe urban losses showed why faster risk-level insights and regional prioritization are critical.

2011 Japan Earthquake

Complex cascading impacts emphasized the value of data-driven risk monitoring and resilient planning.

How This Prediction Model Can Be Used

  • Estimate magnitude and risk class quickly for incoming seismic coordinates.
  • Prioritize response zones (Low / Medium / High) for control rooms and district teams.
  • Support public alerting workflows and pre-positioning of medical and rescue resources.
  • Guide long-term planning by identifying high-risk depth/region behavior patterns.
